In SEARCH mode, use the following geometric definitions to apply the POINT/TURN rules above.

The exact requested target is not currently confirmed visible. Inspect the entire image again before making a navigation decision.

If the exact target is clearly visible:
- s="V", a="POINT"
- p = exact geometric center of the visible target body, not its cap, base, label edge, shadow, or nearby background

Otherwise use s="I" and apply the following decision order strictly.

1. NEAR FULL-BLOCK OVERRIDE — CHECK BEFORE POINT

Before searching for a waypoint, check whether the current forward route is already blocked at close range.

TURN_LEFT or TURN_RIGHT is mandatory when ALL of the following are visually clear:

- a solid wall, partition, furniture surface, or obstacle blocks most of the central forward corridor, approximately x=250 to x=750
- its floor-contact boundary has reached approximately the lower third of the image, roughly y>=700
- no continuous reachable floor bypass is already visible on the left-front or right-front side

When these conditions are satisfied:
- TURN must be used
- POINT is forbidden
- the short residual floor immediately before the blocking surface is not a valid waypoint

Use the side with the clearer reachable opening or greater connected floor space to choose LEFT or RIGHT.

2. POINT GATE

If the near full-block conditions above are not satisfied, search for a visible reachable floor passage.

Use POINT when:
- free floor is continuously connected from the lower-center of the image into the middle region, approximately reaching y<=650, or
- a visible continuous bypass around a local obstacle already exists

A distant wall, future corner, or partition endpoint does not prevent POINT while this connected floor passage still exists.

Place p on a clear mid-distance floor point inside the connected passage.

Before returning POINT, verify:
- p is visibly on floor texture
- p is not on a wall, furniture surface, partition, obstacle, or their boundary
- if a wall-floor contact edge is visible, p must lie on the floor side of that edge
- reaching p must not merely place the robot immediately against a blocking surface

3. TURN FALLBACK

If no connected floor passage reaches the middle region because the route is blocked, use TURN_LEFT or TURN_RIGHT.

After a small turn, return to POINT as soon as a reachable floor passage becomes visible.
